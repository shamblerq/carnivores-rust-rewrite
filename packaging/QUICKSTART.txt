Omnivores Rust @VERSION@
========================

A launcher for Carnivores, Carnivores 2 and Carnivores: Ice Age, rewritten
in Rust, and for their mods. You need your own copies of the games: this
download holds none of their data.

1. Start omnivores-rust (omnivores-rust.exe on Windows).

   On Windows, SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC", as it does for
   any program it has not seen before: choose More info, then Run anyway.

2. Choose "Add games..." to bring in your games and mods: from their
   folders (an old install, a CD, a Wine prefix), from the archives mods
   come in (.zip, and .7z or .rar with 7-Zip installed), or let it find
   them on this computer. Folders and archives can also be dropped on its
   window.

   A mod that holds only the files it changes is laid over a copy of its
   game, as its readme would have you do by hand.

3. To start it like any other program, say yes when it offers to add
   itself to your applications menu (Start menu on Windows) and desktop,
   or choose Settings, then Install. It copies itself to a folder of its
   own, and this folder can then be deleted. Uninstall is on the same
   page, and on Windows in the system's list of installed apps too.

Double-click a game to play it. Right-click one for its own menu entry or
desktop shortcut, and more.

The full manual is README.md, and the project is at
https://github.com/shamblerq/carnivores-rust-rewrite
