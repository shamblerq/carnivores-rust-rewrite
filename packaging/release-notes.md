Ready-built programs for Linux and Windows (64-bit): no need to build from
source. You need your own copies of the games.

| Download | |
|---|---|
| **Linux**: `omnivores-rust-@VERSION@-linux-x86_64.tar.xz` | Unpack it and run `omnivores-rust`. Needs a Linux from 2022 or later (glibc 2.35: Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12, Fedora 36) and Vulkan. |
| **Windows**: `omnivores-rust-@VERSION@-windows-x86_64.zip` | Unpack it and run `omnivores-rust.exe`. Needs Windows 10 or later, and Vulkan or Direct3D 12. |

Then, in the launcher:

1. **Add games…** brings in your games and mods, from their folders, from
   the archives mods come in, or from wherever it finds them on your
   computer.
2. **Settings → Install** (it also offers this on its first start) adds it
   to your applications menu or Start menu and your desktop, and copies it
   to a folder of its own so the download can go.

Windows may warn about a program from an unknown publisher: choose **More
info → Run anyway**. `SHA256SUMS` lists the downloads' checksums.
